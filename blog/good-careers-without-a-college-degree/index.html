27 Good Careers Without a College Degree That Pay $50k+ (2026)

Updated October 2, 2026 · ~7 min read · By Skyra Roadmap

A four-year degree is one path — not the only one. These 27 careers all pay a solid living without requiring a bachelor's degree, and most can be started with a certificate, an associate degree, or a paid apprenticeship. Pay figures are U.S. medians; many people earn well above them with experience.

The hard part isn't finding a good-paying job without college — it's finding one your teen will actually enjoy and stick with. We've grouped these by the kind of person they suit, and at the bottom there's a free 5-minute quiz that matches your teen to the ones that fit how they're wired.

The list: 27 careers that pay without a 4-year degree

How to pick the right one (not just the highest-paying one)

Chasing the biggest number is the most common mistake. A high salary in a job that drains your teen leads to burnout and a restart at 25. Three questions sort this out fast:

  • Hands or screen? Some teens come alive building and fixing real things; others want to work with information and ideas. Trades vs. tech starts here.
  • People or solo? Sales, nursing, and teaching are people-heavy. Coding, machining, and driving give you more quiet. Neither is better — but mismatch is miserable.
  • How soon do they want to earn? Apprenticeships and CDL programs pay within weeks to months. Associate-degree health roles take about two years but pay more. Both beat four years of debt.
Parent tip: Every career above has a clear first step — an apprenticeship, a certificate, or a short program. Have your teen pick two that interest them and spend an afternoon finding the exact local program and what it costs. Real next steps beat endless scrolling.

What about community college and certificates?

Community college is the quiet winner here. Many of the best-paying roles on this list — dental hygiene, sonography, nursing (ADN), radiologic tech — run through two-year community-college programs that cost a fraction of a university and plug straight into a licensed, in-demand career. Frequently asked questions

What is the highest-paying job without a degree?

Among jobs that don't require a four-year degree, commercial pilots, elevator installers, dental hygienists, radiation therapists, and power-line installers regularly clear $85,000–$115,000. Many skilled trades also pass six figures once you're licensed and running your own work.

Are trade jobs a good career in 2026?

Yes. Trades like electrical, plumbing, and HVAC pair strong pay with very low schooling cost (paid apprenticeships), can't be offshored, and face worker shortages as older tradespeople retire — which keeps wages and demand high.

How do I know which no-degree career fits my teen?
